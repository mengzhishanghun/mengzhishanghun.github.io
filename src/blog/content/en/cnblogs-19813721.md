UE: Fixing UMG Size Not Changing During Rendering
===

When you need to render UMG, you may encounter a really annoying situation: the rendered UMG does not change size to match your chosen output resolution

The solution is simple and also annoying: open Project Settings, find the following option, and check it

![](/media-occurrence/blog%3Acnblogs-19813721%3Amedia%3A1)

Or add the following to DefaultEditor.ini

```
[/Script/MovieRenderPipelineEditor.MoviePipelinePIEExecutorSettings]

bResizePIEWindowToOutputResolution=True

```
