===

First, you need a server administrator account to perform this operation

Open CMD, enter p4 typemap, and press Enter

The TypeMap configuration file will open automatically

Copy the following contents into it

The content below extends the TypeMap commonly used for UE based on my practical experience, and currently suits most development environments
