UE: How to Call a DLL
===

> © [mengzhishanghun](https://github.com/mengzhishanghun) · Original article\
> &#x20;First published on [Cnblogs](https://www.cnblogs.com/mengzhishanghun) · Unauthorized reproduction prohibited

***

Let me share my approach. I don't know whether it's correct, but it works, hahaha. I followed the official third-party library setup\
&#x20;![](/media-occurrence/blog%3Acnblogs-19174295%3Amedia%3A1)\
&#x20;I won't go into the whole process of packaging a DLL; I'll just explain what to do once you have the DLL and Lib\
&#x20;Taking a plugin named TestThird created from the official template as an example\
&#x20;Put the dll and lib files in `TestThird\Source\ThirdParty\TestThirdLibrary\x64\Release 然后在`TestThird\Binaries\ThirdParty\TestThirdLibrary\Win64\` and put a dll there\
&#x20;In theory, this step could be improved, for example by copying automatically, but I haven't had time to look into it\
&#x20;After adding them, add the following to the module's Build.cs\
&#x20;![](/media-occurrence/blog%3Acnblogs-19174295%3Amedia%3A2)\
&#x20;Finally, add a .h file to the module with the same name as the dll to declare the callable functions in the dll\
&#x20;![](/media-occurrence/blog%3Acnblogs-19174295%3Amedia%3A3)\
&#x20;If you don't know which functions the DLL contains, download [Dependencies](https://github.com/lucasg/Dependencies)\
&#x20;![](/media-occurrence/blog%3Acnblogs-19174295%3Amedia%3A4)

Drag the DLL into it to inspect it. It's very convenient\
&#x20;Finally, when the module loads, you need to call FPlatformProcess::GetDllHandle. I modified it a little myself, roughly\
&#x20;like this. Don't copy this verbatim: I temporarily changed it for demonstration. It should actually be passed in through windll, macdll, and linuxdll\
&#x20;![](/media-occurrence/blog%3Acnblogs-19174295%3Amedia%3A5)\
&#x20;Don't forget to call FPlatformProcess::FreeDllHandle when the module unloads\
&#x20;![](/media-occurrence/blog%3Acnblogs-19174295%3Amedia%3A6)\
&#x20;Now you can call it. It's straightforward: include the header and call it normally\
&#x20;![](/media-occurrence/blog%3Acnblogs-19174295%3Amedia%3A7)

Reference documentation: [Integrating Third-Party Libraries into Unreal Engine | Unreal Engine 5.4 Documentation | Epic Developer Community (epicgames.com)](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/zh-cn/unreal-engine/integrating-third-party-libraries-into-unreal-engine)
